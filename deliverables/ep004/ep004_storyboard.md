## Visual grammar
- Higgsfield D1–D4 (approved): hand on the wooden seat, hand on the metal leg, the established character
  between a metal block and a wood block, and a vertical blocks plate for the Short.
- Code only: blocks, fingertips, thermometers (no numbers), ice cubes, arrows, the energy-spread dots, labels.
- **Warm gold arrows always point warmer → cooler.** No "cold particles" entering a hand, no frost on
  room-temperature metal, no bare hand on a hot surface (the reverse case is an abstract diagram).
- Every diagram is tagged **"Simplified illustration."**; the melting is tagged "Qualitative animation: not a timed result."
