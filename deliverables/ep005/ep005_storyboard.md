## Game grammar
- Brand names are **typeset in code** and built letter by letter ("morph row"): letter groups grow and
  shrink in place, so ARBY'S → R. B. → ROAST BEEF → RAFFEL BROTHERS → ARBY'S are literal transformations.
  No logos; the R4 roadside sign has blank panels that we letter ourselves.
- **Game board** of four name cards ("4 NAMES · 4 SECRETS"); each solved card collects a gold ORIGIN stamp.
- **Guess cards** flip in during the natural pause after each question; the right one turns gold.
- **Our character** (R2, golden sweater) is the guesser, with a "?" thought bubble that becomes ✓.
- The founders appear only as generic figures **from behind**, tagged **"Illustrative recreation"** beside
  their names. Wendy's home/sign sequence sits in a dashed **IMAGINE** frame with icons only.
