## Accuracy guardrails (from the brief)
- Live self-hearing = air conduction + vibrations through bone and tissue; a microphone records the
  airborne part only (C, E, H).
- No "skull is a subwoofer" claim; no claim that recordings perfectly reproduce what others hear
  (H lists mic/position/room/processing/speaker); no promise that repeated listening fixes anything.
- Demo: same take, EQ only, no pitch shift, matched loudness, labelled as an illustration; nothing
  claims to reproduce what any viewer hears internally.
