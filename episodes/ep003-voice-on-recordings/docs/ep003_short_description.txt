Why does your voice sound weird on recordings? While you talk, you also hear it through vibrations in your own head, and an ordinary microphone only records the sound through the air. Full explanation in the main video (set it as this Short's related video in YouTube Studio).

Sources: Chalmers University of Technology; The University of Tokyo; Royal Society Open Science (2023); Imperial College London. Links are in the main video's description.
