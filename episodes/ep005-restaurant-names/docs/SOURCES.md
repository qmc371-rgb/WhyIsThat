   https://www.tacobell.com/newsroom/taco-bell-celebrates-60-years
   > "In 1962, Glen Bell opened the first Taco Bell in Downey, California …"
5. **Starbucks, "Our Name."** https://about.starbucks.com/history/our-name/ 🔁
   Per search excerpts of the page: Gordon Bowker first proposed "Pequod"; Terry Heckler objected; while
   looking at old mining-camp names near Mt. Rainier, Heckler "came across 'Starbo,' which eventually led
   the team back to where they'd started. In Moby Dick, the name of the first mate on the Pequod was …
   Starbuck." Starbucks' company profile (Jan 2026) says the company is named after the first mate in
   Melville's *Moby-Dick*; its About Us page says the name evokes the seafaring tradition of the early
   coffee traders.
